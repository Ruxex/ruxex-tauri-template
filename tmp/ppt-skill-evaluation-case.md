# PPT skill evaluation case

## Scenario
Create a Japanese-language, 6-slide decision deck for the fictional Kairo Works executive team. They must decide whether to expand the company's direct-to-consumer launch in Q1 2027. This is synthetic data for evaluating slide quality; do not present any details as real-world facts.

## Audience and decision
- Audience: CEO, CFO, and Head of Sales; familiar with the product but short on time.
- Decision: approve or reject a controlled D2C expansion, and specify the conditions for proceeding.
- Main message: approve a capped 90-day pilot, not a full-scale rollout, because demand is promising but the margin and return-rate evidence is not yet stable.

## Source data (all amounts are JPY)

| Quarter | D2C orders | Net revenue | Gross margin | Return rate | Paid media spend |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026 Q1 | 1,200 | 18,000,000 | 42% | 4.0% | 3,000,000 |
| 2026 Q2 | 1,500 | 22,500,000 | 41% | 4.5% | 4,200,000 |
| 2026 Q3 | 1,800 | 27,000,000 | 39% | 5.0% | 5,400,000 |
| 2026 Q4 | 2,000 | 30,000,000 | 38% | 5.5% | 6,000,000 |

Additional verified facts:
- Pilot budget cap: JPY 12,000,000 over 90 days, including media and temporary support.
- Fulfillment capacity: at most 2,400 orders per quarter without a warehouse change.
- Current blended acquisition cost in Q4: JPY 3,000 per order (media spend / orders).
- Finance's pilot guardrails: gross margin at least 40%, return rate no more than 4.5%, and acquisition cost no more than JPY 3,200 per order.
- No cohort-level repeat-purchase data is available. Do not infer lifetime value or claim causality.

## Required slide sequence
1. Decision and recommendation, with the pilot cap and the three guardrails.
2. Evidence of demand: show the order and revenue trend without claiming profitable growth.
3. Unit economics: show the margin and return-rate deterioration alongside acquisition cost; explain the tension.
4. A 90-day pilot design with owners, checkpoints at days 30/60/90, and explicit stop / continue rules tied to the guardrails.
5. Risks and mitigations, including capacity, returns, and missing repeat-purchase evidence.
6. Decision requested today and the next three actions.

## Content and quality constraints
- Use Japanese, concise executive language, and a clear takeaway title on every slide.
- Preserve every supplied number and unit exactly; derived calculations must be arithmetically correct and labeled as derived.
- Do not invent market size, customer quotes, benchmarks, sources, owners' names, or forecast results.
- Use at least one readable data visualization and avoid dense paragraphs.
- Include a compact source note: “出典：Kairo Works社内テストデータ（2026 Q1–Q4）、経営企画作成。架空データ。”
- Make the recommendation and decision rules understandable without narration.
