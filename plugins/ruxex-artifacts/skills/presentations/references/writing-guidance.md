# Slide writing

Write titles that describe the slide's subject or state a finding the evidence
supports. Use short body copy, concrete labels, and natural sentences. Remove
filler, unsupported claims, repeated headlines, slogans, and presenter narration.

Keep comparisons explicit: name the period, measure, and baseline. Distinguish
actuals, forecasts, assumptions, and recommendations. Use active wording when
it reads naturally, and keep technical terms when precision requires them.

Review each title with the body copy. A title can be concise without being a
slogan or an instruction. Preserve the evidence and caveats the audience needs.
