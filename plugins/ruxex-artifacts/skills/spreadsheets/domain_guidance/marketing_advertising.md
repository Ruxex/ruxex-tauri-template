# Marketing and advertising guidance

Use for campaign, funnel, lead, CRM, attribution, and advertising-performance tasks. For a marketing company's budget or valuation, use [Finance guidance](financial_models.md) and add relevant marketing metric definitions.

- Distinguish extract freshness from analysis time. Preserve the source date range, attribution window, platform, and comparison basis.
- Keep raw campaign or CRM exports intact. Put authorized cleaning, exclusions, and category mappings in derived fields or a prepared area and document the rules.
- Preserve metric grain, denominator, and definition. Define abbreviations such as CPC when they are not obvious from context.
- Calculate rates and costs from matching aggregate totals when that reflects the requested metric. Do not average record-level ratios unless the requested method calls for it.
- Use target-based emphasis only when the source supplies the target and the better/worse direction.
- Add a chart only when it clarifies the requested trend, comparison, or funnel. Keep its source ranges traceable.
- Identify source platforms and extract dates near the inputs. Label assumptions and keep output periods and units clear.
