# Cover design

Use this guidance when creating or redesigning a cover. Keep an existing cover
unless the requested edit requires changing it. Count the cover toward the
requested slide total; do not add one when it is unnecessary.

Start with the user's design, audience, and available images. Without supplied
direction, keep the title slide minimal. A solid background, source photograph,
or relevant illustration can all work. Make the subject clear and the title
readable against its background. Design the cover itself; do not use a
miniature webpage or slide as the whole cover.

Match a supplied reference's composition, including intentional asymmetry,
spacing, and image margins. Otherwise, give images and text the space they need.
Preserve logo and original artwork proportions. Crop only when it will not
remove required detail; otherwise fit the whole image inside its frame.

Keep titles editable. Put source credits in speaker notes unless the user,
license, or audience needs them on the slide. Do not add comments about image
generation or design quality. Do not present generated scenes as records of
real events or objects.
