# Scientific research guidance

Use for research observations, experiments, laboratory measurements, surveys, statistical analysis, and reproducibility tasks. Use Finance guidance for financial forecasts at a research organization.

- Preserve raw observations. Use one variable per column and one observation per row for analytical tables when possible.
- Keep original measurements separate from cleaning, transformations, and derived results. Record protocol and cleaning steps when needed to reproduce the analysis.
- Include units in headers or clear metadata. Keep units consistent within a column and preserve the original unit and value when conversions are made.
- Do not rely on formatting or color alone to encode data. Use labeled values for flags and missingness.
- Keep data tables simple and machine-readable. Avoid merged cells and multi-row headers in raw or processed data intended for analysis tools.
- For randomization or simulation, document the method, inputs, and reproducibility requirements. Do not introduce random processes without request.
- Show meaningful calculation steps and independently cross-check consequential calculations where possible. Report evaluator limitations.
- Preserve source protocol and definitions. Distinguish assumptions, missing observations, and measured zero values.
